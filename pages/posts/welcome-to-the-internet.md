# Welcome to the Internet, Netscape!

Support for Netscape Navigator has been added! 

Right now, it is very minimal, but the [Home](/#) and [About](/#about.html) pages are accessible. The [Blog](/#blog.html) is currently not accessible as a rework in the way posts are loaded is necessary. 

The curent minimum requirements for the page to load are a web browser with frames and JavaScript 1.0 support, which should cover all post-Netscape Navigator 2.0 browsers. The target will remain at browsers implementing Internet Explorer 6 features, with full compatibility targeting IE 8.

In other news, page transitions and animations have been added, though they're are slow on IE.

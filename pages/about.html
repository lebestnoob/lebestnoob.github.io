The Beginnings

I began my journey with Windify as a way to fool classmates into believing I had merged Windows 10 and ChromeOS. At the time, I created shortcuts to a website with favicons set to Windows icons, but wanting to sell the effect better, I decided to recreate the "classic" start menu. This eventually became a recreation of the Windows 10 settings app, and where I obtained my first experience writing mediocre CSS and JavaScript.
